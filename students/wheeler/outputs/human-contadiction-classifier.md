## Method Notes

- Word shares are counted from the speaker-labeled lines in each transcript. They're approximate, because auto-transcription splits and merges speech unevenly.
- The percentages are estimates. They weigh how often a view is stated, how emphatically, and whether his actions match it.
- Some apparent contradictions were left out because he resolves them himself in the transcripts. One example is small, narrowly scoped crates alongside a single facade crate that re-exports everything (C3 7:34, C3 11:41).
