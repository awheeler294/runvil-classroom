## Objective 

Humans are complicated and commonly hold contradictory views on any given subject. Given the input conversations, find contradictory views that the major participants express.
The major participants are anyone who contributes at least 25% of the conversation.

## Methodology
For each major participant, list issues they express contradictory views on. 
Summarize the contradictory views, and assign each one a numerical percentage estimating how strongly they hold that view. Percentages should be whole numbers and sum up to 100 for each issue.

## Output
The output should be in markdown format. Include a list if input files near the beginning.
